Titles take the form *"Reserve Bank of India (&lt;entity&gt; — Responsible Business Conduct)
Third/Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026"*. Commencement is identical across all nine:

> "These Directions shall come into effect from January 1, 2027."

**The correction this table forces.** The compliance notes described a single "new Section L
(paras 454A–454AB)" as though it applied across the board. Section L / 454A–454AB is the
numbering for **Commercial Banks and Small Finance Banks only**. Every other category has its
own section letter and its own paragraph numbers, and they are *not* letter-aligned across
categories. Citing "454A" at an NBFC is wrong — the NBFC equivalent is 100A.

## The provisions this project actually relies on

### Contact window, 08:00–19:00 — CONFIRMED, at a different paragraph than recorded

> "An employee / recovery agent shall contact / visit the borrower / guarantor only between
> 08:00 hours and 19:00 hours."
> — **454Y(4)** (Commercial Banks, RBI/2026-27/223); identical at **454Y(4)** (SFB,
> RBI/2026-2027/224) and **100Y(4)** (NBFC, RBI/2026-27/230)

The compliance notes cited this as 454T.2. Wrong — it is 454Y(4).

This is the primary citation for the 08:00–19:00 IST product invariant. Two things in the
drafting matter for implementation: the window binds **contact *or* visit**, and it is
written as a permission ("only between"), not as a prohibition on a range — so the state
machine should treat outside-window as denied by default rather than checking against a
blocked list.

### Contact only the borrower or guarantor — CONFIRMED, paragraph corrected

> "An employee / recovery agent shall discuss the matters related to the loan dues and
> collection / recovery thereof only with the borrower / guarantor, as applicable."
> — **454Y(1)** (Commercial Banks); **100Y(1)** (NBFC)

Recorded as 454T.1. Wrong — it is 454Y(1).

### "Excessively calling", with no numeric cap — CONFIRMED, paragraph corrected

> "Excessively calling / messaging to the borrower / guarantor and / or calling / messaging
> outside the prescribed hours"
> — **454Z(4)** (Commercial Banks); **100Z(4)** (NBFC), listed as a harsh practice

Recorded as 454U.3. Wrong — it is 454Z(4).

**The substance holds, and that is the important part.** RBI names no number; the prohibition
is qualitative. The repo rule *never cite a numeric call cap to RBI* is confirmed correct
against the regulator's own words. One detail worth carrying into the design: this paragraph
covers **messaging**, not only calling — so the 7-in-7 policy choice has to be applied to SMS
and WhatsApp attempts as well, not just to voice.

### Call recording, six-month retention — CONFIRMED

> records preserved "for a period of six months from the date the call was made, or in cases
> which are sub judice, till they are disposed of."
> — **100P** (NBFC, RBI/2026-27/230)

### IIBF certification for recovery agents — CONFIRMED

> agents must obtain a "certificate from Indian Institute of Banking and Finance (IIBF) after
> completing the training programme for Debt Recovery Agents."
> — **100I** (NBFC, RBI/2026-27/230)

### Grievance as a hard stop — **NOT SUPPORTED. Claim withdrawn.**

The compliance notes asserted:

> "a lodged grievance is a hard stop — the case may not be forwarded for recovery until the
> grievance is disposed of (454J), exception only with *evidence* of frivolous filing"

**454J says no such thing.** Verbatim:

> "A bank, based on the instructions mentioned in these Directions, shall put in place a code
> of conduct for recovery agents and its own employees. Where a recovery agency has been
> engaged, the bank shall obtain an undertaking from the recovery agency that its recovery
> agents agree to abide by the code of conduct."
> — **454J** (Commercial Banks, RBI/2026-27/223), heading *L.2.3 Code of Conduct*

454J is the code-of-conduct paragraph. Neighbouring headings: 454G *L.1 Policy*, 454H *L.2.1
Due diligence*, 454I *L.2.2 Training*, 454K–454N *L.3.1 Disclosure of information on recovery
agencies*.

Searched for and not found:

- No clause in the Commercial Banks, SFB or NBFC directions requires a complaint or grievance
  to be disposed of **before** an account is forwarded to a recovery agency, or before
  recovery may continue. **ABSENT** in all three.
- The word **"frivolous" does not appear** in the Commercial Banks direction at all.
- Grievance redressal does exist, at **454AA**, but it governs handling complaints *during and
  after* recovery. It is not a bar on recovery action.

**Consequence for the build.** "Dispute raised → freeze and route to a human" stays in the
design, but it is relabelled **a policy choice**, exactly like the 7-in-7 cap — not an RBI
requirement. Presenting it as RBI-mandated would have been a false citation to a regulator, in
a submission whose entire pitch is that its compliance claims are checkable. This is the most
valuable thing gate 0.6 produced, and it would not have surfaced without the primary text.

## The rule actually in force today (until 1 Jan 2027)

**RBI/2022-23/108**, DOR.ORG.REC.65/21.04.158/2022-23, 12 August 2022 — *"Outsourcing of
Financial Services – Responsibilities of regulated entities employing Recovery Agents"* —
[Id=12378](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12378&Mode=0)

REs shall ensure agents do not resort to (paragraph 2, verbatim):

> "persistently calling the borrower and/ or calling the borrower before 8:00 a.m. and after
> 7:00 p.m. for recovery of overdue loans"

<!-- CORRECTED 4 Sept 2026: the trailing "for recovery of overdue loans" was truncated here
     while the fragment was labelled verbatim. It is in paragraph 2. The phrase is the scope
     limiter tying the window to LOAN recovery, so restoring it narrows the rule and supports
     the merchant-versus-lender distinction this project relies on. See docs/compliance-india.md. -->

> "intimidation or harassment of any kind, either verbal or physical"

> acts "intended to humiliate publicly or intrude upon the privacy of the debtors' family
> members, referees and friends"

> "sending inappropriate messages either on mobile or through social media"

> "making threatening and/ or anonymous calls"

> "making false and misleading representations"

So both instruments support 08:00–19:00, and both describe repetition qualitatively —
"persistently" in 2022, "excessively" in 2026 — with no number attached in either.

**Today's binding instrument is the 2022 circular; the 2026 directions bind from 1 Jan 2027.**
A submission dated 5 Sept 2026 should cite the 2022 circular as current law and the 2026
directions as incoming, not the other way round.

## Who this binds — unchanged, and still the honest framing

Everything above binds **Regulated Entities**. The scope clause is explicit:

> "The provisions under this Section shall apply to recovery of loan dues by an AIFI from
> borrowers in default, including taking possession of a security."
> — RBI/2026-27/229

A merchant recovering its own failed UPI Autopay mandate is not an RE, and a failed mandate
is not a *loan due*. The correct claim stays exactly as `CLAUDE.md` has it: 08:00–19:00 IST is
adopted as a **product invariant and de-facto standard**, which happens to make the product
sellable to an RE unchanged — not as a rule that binds the demo merchant.
